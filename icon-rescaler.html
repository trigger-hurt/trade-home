How this works

Make face icons from your art and save them as a DMI. Here's what each part does.

Two modes

Face Icons (top-left) makes square icons at the Size you pick. Playby switches to 238×400 character portraits — the crop and output become that shape. Each image keeps its own crop for each mode, so flipping between them never disturbs the other.

Adding images

Drag image files onto the page, paste one with Ctrl+V, or click Add. You can add a lot at once — up to 150. They show up in the list on the left and along the bottom.

Picking the face

Click an image to open it. The square on it is your crop. Drag it over the face, or pull a corner to resize. It always stays square.

Turn it — use the Rotate buttons or slider if a face is sideways or upside-down. It comes out straight.

Look closer — scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in. Hold the middle mouse button to drag around.

The settings (bottom row)

Size — how big the icon is.

Sharpen — keeps small faces looking crisp instead of blurry.

Mode / Mix — leave these alone. The defaults are fine.

Crop — another way to size the crop box.

Include — untick to skip an image without deleting it.

Colors

Off keeps every color. On uses fewer colors and makes a smaller file. The number beside it is how many colors to keep (1–255). Go too low and flat art starts to look blocky.

Per img / Shared only changes the preview on screen. The DMI always uses your number.

Transparency

Keep leaves it alone. Remove fills the see-through parts with a color (white by default), so nothing's transparent.

Keep/Remove is for the image you're on. All/None does every image at once.

Names & saving

The boxes under each preview are the icon names in the DMI. Rename them however you like — matching names turn red so you can fix them.

Copy copies the current icon so you can paste it. Export ZIP saves each icon as its own PNG. Export DMI makes one .dmi file with all your icons.

Tips

Lots of different art styles in one DMI? Turn Colors Off so nothing looks wrong.

Start at 255 colors and only lower it if the art still looks fine.

Images

Drop image files here, paste with Ctrl+V, or click Add.

You can batch upload by adding files, or, copy and paste files into here, and it'll stack so it's faster.
